Engineering Ethos

How I Think & Build

The bridge between mechanical wrenching and distributed systems architecture.

First-Principles Diagnostics

Whether isolating an engine misfire or diagnosing a high-load latency spike across microservices, I don't guess. I break systems down to their fundamentals, inspect the tolerances, and solve the root cause rather than patching symptoms.

Resilience Over Complexity

The best architecture is simple, decoupled, and self-healing. I design gateways and event streams that protect upstream services, handle unexpected traffic surges without breaking a sweat, and never wake teams up at 3 AM.

Continuous Craft & AI Velocity

I love adopting modern tools that accelerate execution — from Generative AI developer agents to rapid POC prototyping — allowing small, focused teams to achieve monumental engineering velocity.
